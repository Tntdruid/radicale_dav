Share a calendar

  1. Open the calendar manager and sign in with your mailbox address and mail password.
  2. Open your calendar's sharing controls and add the other mailbox address.
  3. Use the exact mailbox address on your domain and save the share.
  4. The other user opens the manager, selects Incoming Shares, and accepts the share.
  5. They enable and unhide the share if those options are shown, then refresh their calendar list.

Shares are read-only by default. Both users must use their mailbox addresses and mail passwords; DirectAdmin passwords are not used.

Most apps only need the server address plus your username/ password -- they'll discover the calendar and address book automatically (via /.well-known/caldav and /.well-known/carddav). First connection creates them if they don't exist yet.

Enter a valid mailbox address, for example alice@example.com.
