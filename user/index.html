Calendar & Contacts (CalDAV/CardDAV)

Your calendar and address book use the same login as your email account (mailbox address + mail password). Enter your mailbox address below to get your URLs:

Open calendar manager

Use the calendar manager to share a calendar with another authenticated mailbox. Shares are read-only by default, and the recipient must accept the share before it appears.

Most apps only need the server address plus your username/ password -- they'll discover the calendar and address book automatically (via /.well-known/caldav and /.well-known/carddav). First connection creates them if they don't exist yet.

Enter a valid mailbox address, for example alice@example.com.
