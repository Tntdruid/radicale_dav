Radicale CalDAV/CardDAV

This plugin gives every mailbox (user@domain.tld) a CalDAV/CardDAV account, authenticated directly against Dovecot -- same login as email, no separate password.

End users

Regular DirectAdmin users find their calendar/contacts URLs under their own Calendar & Contacts tab (/CMD_PLUGINS/radicale_dav).

Admin reference

Radicale config/etc/radicale/config
Collections storage/var/lib/radicale/collections
Servicesystemctl status radicale
Cleanup hook/usr/local/directadmin/scripts/custom/email_destroy_post.sh
Reverse proxyevery domain's own /caldav (see config/apache-vhost-template-snippet.conf)
